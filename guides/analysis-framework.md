# Analysis Framework

**Purpose:** Transform raw questionnaire responses into a coherent personality architecture

**Time needed:** 45-60 minutes (self-guided) or instant (AI-assisted)

---

## How to Use This Guide

### Option A: Self-Analysis
Work through each section below, using your questionnaire responses to fill in each component of your personality architecture.

### Option B: AI Analysis
Skip this manual process. Share your thought dump + questionnaire with AI and ask: "Analyze my responses and generate my personality architecture using the framework in guides/analysis-framework.md"

### Option C: Peer Analysis
Work with a partner to interview each other and map out personality architectures together.

## 1. Energy System Map

**Goal:** Understand what fills you up vs. what drains you

### What Energizes You (Peak Moments)

Look at **Q1 (Peak Moments)** - what did you select?

- If **Learning/discovering**: You're energized by **acquisition** (gaining new capabilities)
- If **Building/launching**: You're energized by **creation** (making things exist in the world)
- If **Teaching/sharing**: You're energized by **exchange** (meaningful connections)
- If **Physical/sensory**: You're energized by **experience** (embodied presence)

**Write:** *I'm energized by:* _______________________

### Home Base (Recharge Mode)

Look at **Q7 (Home Base Mode)** - what did you select?

This is your **recharge state** - the mode you could stay in longest without draining.

**Important distinction:**
- What you DO in free time (Q2) might be different from your HOME BASE
- If they're different: You create in discretionary time but recharge in a different mode
- This is KEY: Creation might satisfy you but not restore you

**Write:** *My home base (recharge mode) is:* _______________________

**Write:** *My discretionary time default is:* _______________________

**If different, note:** *Creation is satisfying but not restorative. ______ restores me.*

### What Drains You

Look at **Q3 (Energy Drain)** - what did you select?

Common patterns:
- **Volume without integration**: Too many things at once, none connected
- **Public performance pressure**: Everything has to count/be seen
- **Comparison loops**: Measuring against others or future ideal
- **One-sided relationships**: Takers, performative people (Q10)
- **Mode stagnation**: Stuck in one mode too long (Q8)
- **Physical stagnation**: No movement/place-change
- **Work structure**: Constraints fighting your operating system (Q9)
- **Rest guilt**: Can't recharge because restoration feels like failure (Q22)

**Write:** *My primary drain sources are:*
1. _______________________
2. _______________________
3. _______________________

**Key insight to look for:**
> My drain isn't from activity type - it's from [volume/pressure/comparison/stagnation/etc.]

## 2. Core Drive & Identity

**Goal:** Understand what's really driving your goals and pursuits

### Primary Drive

Look at **Q5 (Revenue Drive)** - even if you didn't select revenue goals, what's motivating your multi-faceted pursuits?

Common drives:
- **Identity diversification**: Not wanting to be just one thing
- **Financial freedom**: Multiple sources = security
- **Proof of viability**: Each identity needs to "work"
- **Autonomy**: Building things that sustain themselves

**Write:** *My core drive is:* _______________________

### Identity Architecture

Look at **Q6 (Depth vs. Breadth)** - what did you select?

- If **excellence in a few**: You're a depth specialist
- If **capability across many**: You're a breadth generalist
- If **one anchor + many satellites**: You're an anchored polymath
- If **still figuring out**: You're fighting societal pressure vs. natural wiring

**Write:** *My identity architecture is:* _______________________

**Common pattern for polymaths:**
> I want capability across many areas. I'm not conflicted - I'm fighting societal pressure to specialize. My actual wiring: One anchor (my deep expertise) + Many satellites (explorations that feed my range).

## 3. Decision Filter: The "Worth It" Test

**Goal:** Understand what makes something worth pursuing for YOU

Look at **Q4 (Worth Filter)** - what did you select?

Build your personal "worth it" formula:

**Something is worth pursuing if it:**
- [ ] Could become a revenue stream
- [ ] Fills a skill/knowledge gap
- [ ] Connects to community/culture
- [ ] Produces something tangible to share

**Write your formula:** *For me, something is worth pursuing if it:* _______________________

**Pattern to notice:**
Do you pursue things for ONE reason, or do they need to check MULTIPLE boxes?

**Example:** "Something is worth pursuing if it fills a skill gap AND produces something tangible to share AND could become a revenue stream. I rarely pursue things for JUST one reason."

## 4. Depth vs. Breadth Resolution

**Goal:** Resolve the tension between specialization and generalization

Look at **Q6 (Depth vs. Breadth)** and **Q14 (Completion)**

### Specialization Pattern

If you selected "excellence in a few":
- You complete things when: _______________________
- You measure success by: depth of mastery
- You're satisfied with: being truly expert in narrow domains

### Generalization Pattern

If you selected "capability across many":
- You complete things when: novelty wears off, or you've learned what you needed
- You measure success by: range of capabilities
- You're satisfied with: "good enough" at 10 things > excellent at 3

### Anchored Polymath Pattern

If you selected "one anchor + many satellites":
- Your anchor (deep expertise): _______________________
- Your satellites (explorations): _______________________
- This provides: credibility + income from anchor, identity diversification from satellites

**Write:** *I'm a _______________________ [specialist/generalist/anchored polymath]*

**Key insight:**
> I complete things when: [it's launched / I've learned what I needed / novelty wears off / it evolves into next version]

## 5. Social Architecture

**Goal:** Understand who energizes vs. drains you, and who you create for

### Who You Cut Off

Look at **Q10 (Exhausting People Patterns)** - what did you select?

**Write:** *People who drain me:*
- _______________________
- _______________________

**Opposite pattern:** *People who energize me have these qualities:*
- Reciprocity (they give as much as they take)
- Authenticity (genuine, not performative)
- Respect my multiplicity (get my full range)
- Resolution-oriented (not just drama/complaining)

### Who You Create For

Look at **Q11 (Audience)** - what did you select?

- **Specific people you know**: Your community, people like you
- **Past version of yourself**: Helping others who are where you were
- **Community you're building**: Creating something collective
- **Validation/proof**: Making it real by having others see it

**Write:** *I create for:* _______________________

**Pattern to notice:**
If you create for "community you're building" → You're not seeking validation, you're building something collective. This explains why you want to:
- Expand into multiple cities (physical presence)
- Host events/walks (gathering the community)
- Teach (expanding the community)
- Create products (tangible culture artifacts)

### Teaching/Sharing Energy

Look at **Q12 (Teaching Energy)** - what did you select?

- **Energized**: You're a natural extrovert in teaching mode
- **Satisfied but depleted**: You're an introvert who teaches well but needs recovery
- **Context-dependent**: You're a selective connector - energized by reciprocal exchange, drained by one-way performance

**Write:** *Teaching/sharing _______________________ me when _______________________*

## 6. Mode System

**Goal:** Map your different operating modes and how you switch between them

### Your Operating Modes

Common modes:
1. **Builder/Creator** - Making, launching, producing
2. **Learner/Explorer** - Discovering, trying, being a beginner
3. **Teacher/Sharer** - Coaching, explaining, guiding
4. **Experiencer/Wanderer** - Traveling, walking, sensing, collecting stories

Look at **Q7 (Home Base)** and **Q2 (Natural Pull)**:

**Write:** *I operate in these modes:*
1. _______________________ (Home Base - recharge)
2. _______________________ (Discretionary time default)
3. _______________________
4. _______________________

### Mode Switching Rhythm

Look at **Q8 (Mode Duration)** - what did you select?

- **Hours**: You need variety within a single day
- **1-2 days**: You can have dedicated days but then need to switch
- **A week**: You can deep-dive into one mode, then shift
- **Varies wildly**: Sometimes weeks in one mode, sometimes hours - no fixed pattern

**Write:** *My mode switching rhythm:* _______________________

**Key insight:**
> I need to switch contexts regularly or I feel stuck. The variety itself is the system. [OR] I can deep-dive into one mode for extended periods.

## 7. Key Tensions

**Goal:** Identify the internal conflicts and transitions you're navigating

### Tension 1: Building as Processing vs. Bandwidth Competition

Look at **Q17 (Inner Work)** - did you select multiple options?

If you selected "building IS processing" AND "they compete for bandwidth":

**Insight:** Your external productivity is literally your therapy mechanism. But the volume you're creating is also preventing the quiet internal processing you need. You're using creation to heal while the creation exhausts you.

**Write if this resonates:** *I'm using creation to heal while the creation exhausts me.*

### Tension 2: Future vs. Present

Look at **Q21 (Time Orientation)** - what did you select?

- **Future-building primary**: Driven by becoming, trajectory matters most
- **Present-experiencing primary**: Want to feel alive now, daily experience matters most
- **Constant tension**: Torn between becoming and being
- **They feed each other**: Future informs present, present informs future

**Write:** *My future/present orientation:* _______________________

**If "constant tension":** Your home base might be experiencer/wanderer (recharge through being) but your current reality is achievement-driven (focused on becoming). The life you want vs. the life you're living = misaligned.

### Tension 3: Achievement Culture vs. Integrated Life

Look at **Q22 (Current Reality)** - what did you select?

If you selected multiple "achievement culture" markers:
- Work dominates everything
- Guilt when not producing
- External pressure to achieve

**Insight:** You're IN achievement culture but WANT integrated life.

**Write:**

*I'm transitioning FROM:*
- Work dominates, guilt when not producing, external validation

*TO:*
- Work as one part, wandering as valid, multiple identities coexisting

### Tension 4: Public vs. Private

Look at **Q19 (Quality Bar)** - if you selected "quality in output, scrappy in process":

**Potential problem:** Are you holding your PROCESS to OUTPUT standards? Feeling like everything needs to be "launch-ready"?

**Write if this resonates:** *I'm holding my process to public standards, making exploration exhausting. Everything I do has to "count" publicly.*

### Tension 5: Work Structure

Look at **Q9 (Work Tension)** - what did you select?

**Write:** *My work tension is:* _______________________

Common insight: It's not that work is bad - it's that current work structure doesn't fit. You need remote/flexible work that integrates into multi-dimensional life, doesn't consume all bandwidth.

## Synthesize: Your Personality Type (Named)

Now combine all the above components into a coherent personality type.

### Template:

**"The [Grounding Element] [Operating Pattern] in [Current Transition]"**

**Examples:**
- "The Grounded Cultural Polymath in Achievement Culture Transition"
- "The Family-Anchored Builder in Balance-Seeking Mode"
- "The Wandering Creator in Integration Phase"

### Components:

**Grounding Element** (from Q20 Family, Q16 Cultural Roots):
- Family-anchored
- Culturally-rooted
- Community-centered
- Independent/solo
- etc.

**Operating Pattern** (from Q6 Depth vs. Breadth, Q1 Energy):
- Polymath (broad range)
- Specialist (deep focus)
- Builder (creation-driven)
- Explorer (learning-driven)
- Connector (relationship-driven)

**Current Transition** (from Q22 Current Reality, Section 7 Tensions):
- Achievement culture transition
- Balance-seeking
- Integration phase
- Identity expansion
- Career pivot
- etc.

*"The _______________________ _______________________ in _______________________"*
