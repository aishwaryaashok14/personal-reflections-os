# Operating System Design Guide

**Purpose:** Design daily/weekly/monthly systems that work WITH your personality architecture

**Time needed:** 45-60 minutes

**Prerequisite:** Complete the Analysis Framework first to understand your personality architecture

---

## Core Principle

Your operating system should match YOUR wiring, not generic productivity advice.

**Most productivity systems assume:**
- More is better
- Work-life balance (separate domains)
- Single focus/specialization
- External accountability
- Fixed schedules

**Your personal OS should:**
- Honor your actual bandwidth
- Enable work-life integration
- Support your depth/breadth preference
- Use internal alignment
- Flex with your rhythms

---

## The Three-Track System

Most people with multi-dimensional lives need THREE parallel tracks, not one master schedule:

### Track 1: ANCHOR (Sustaining)
**Purpose:** Financial stability + one identity anchor
**Examples:** Day job, main business, core expertise
**Boundary:** This is ONE identity, not your whole self
**Time:** 35-45 hours/week typically

### Track 2: EXPLORATION (Becoming)
**Purpose:** Identity diversification, capability building, curiosity
**Examples:** Learning new skills, side projects, creative pursuits, community building
**Boundary:** Not everything needs to monetize or complete
**Time:** 5-15 hours/week

### Track 3: RESTORATION (Being)
**Purpose:** Recharge, ground, connect, experience
**Examples:** Family, movement, travel, rest, hobbies without purpose
**Boundary:** This is NOT wasted time - it's infrastructure
**Time:** 15-25 hours/week

## Step 5: The "Counts vs. Doesn't Count" Framework

**Problem:** Everything you do has to "count" publicly = constant performance = exhaustion

**Solution:** Create two categories

### PUBLIC CREATION (Counts)
**Definition:** Must meet quality bar, contributes to community/revenue/identity

**Examples from your life:**
- _______________________
- _______________________
- _______________________

**Your Limit:** _____ public launches per month maximum
(Recommended: 2-3 max)

**Why limit?** More visibility = more expectation = more pressure = less space for being

### PRIVATE EXPLORATION (Doesn't Count)
**Definition:** Scrappy, messy, just for you, no one needs to see it

**Examples for you:**
- _______________________
- _______________________
- _______________________

**Your Requirement:** _____ % of creation time should be PRIVATE
(Recommended: 50%)

**Why required?** This is where processing happens without performance pressure

## Step 9: Daily/Weekly/Monthly Practices

### Daily Check-In (2 minutes)

**Instead of "what did I accomplish?", ask:**

- Did I move my body today? [ ]
- Did I create something (even if messy/unseen)? [ ]
- Did I connect with someone meaningful? [ ]
- Did I experience something (not just produce)? [ ]

**If yes to 2+ = successful day**

### Weekly Review (10 minutes)

**Every Sunday, count:**

- Days with morning restoration: ___/7
- Private creation (not for public): ___/7
- Public creation (launching/posting): ___/7
- Meaningful reciprocal connections: ___
- One-sided draining interactions: ___
- Times I said "no": ___
- Mode switches: ___
- Days stuck in one mode: ___/7

**Look for patterns:**
- If depleted days >3 → Identify common trigger
- If private creation = 0 → Schedule it
- If said no = 0 → Boundaries too weak
- If mode switches <10 → Too much stagnation

### Monthly Assessment (20 minutes)

**Score 1-10 on:**

- How often I felt guilty for not producing: ___/10
- How much private (unseen) creation I did: ___/10
- How many opportunities I declined: ___/10
- How present I was during restoration: ___/10
- How balanced my three tracks felt: ___/10
- How alive I felt this month: ___/10

**Goal:** Not perfection, but movement toward aligned living

### Quarterly Review (60 minutes)

**Every 3 months:**

1. **Review personality architecture** - Has anything changed?
2. **Active Queue refresh** - Complete/pause/continue current items, move 2-3 from Someday
3. **Track balance check** - Are all three tracks present?
4. **Bandwidth audit** - Am I over/under capacity?
5. **Do More/Less update** - What needs adjusting?
