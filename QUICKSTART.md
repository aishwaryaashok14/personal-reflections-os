## 5-Minute Quick Start (Non-Interactive)

### Step 1: Brain Dump (60 seconds)
Open a blank doc and write:
- 3 things you want to do
- 3 things you're proud of doing
- 1 thing that's overwhelming you right now

### Step 2: Energy Check (60 seconds)
Answer these:
- What made you feel most alive in the past month?
- What drained you most?
- What would you do with 3 free hours today?

### Step 3: The One Question (60 seconds)
**"Am I overwhelmed because I'm doing the wrong things, or too many of the right things?"**

If **wrong things** → You need to prune/quit things
If **too many right things** → You need to design a sustainable operating system

### Step 4: First Action (120 seconds)
**If wrong things:**
- Pick 1 thing to say no to this week
- Pick 1 thing to move from "active" to "someday"

**If too many right things:**
- Go to [templates/01-thought-dump.md](./templates/01-thought-dump.md)
- Spend 30 minutes completing it
- Then move to the questionnaire

## Common First Questions

**Q: Do I need to complete everything in one sitting?**
A: No! Do thought dump today, questionnaire tomorrow, analysis next week. Spread it out.

**Q: What if I don't know how to answer a question?**
A: Pick the closest option and add notes. Or skip it and return later. Nothing is final.

**Q: Can I change my answers later?**
A: Yes! This is a living document. Review quarterly and update as you evolve.

**Q: What if my answers seem contradictory?**
A: Perfect! Contradictions reveal tensions you're navigating. That's valuable data.

**Q: Do I have to do the full 90-day implementation?**
A: No. Start with Month 1. See what happens. Adjust from there.

**Q: What if I don't have "accomplishments" to list?**
A: List experiences, moments that mattered, things you tried, people you connected with. It all counts.
