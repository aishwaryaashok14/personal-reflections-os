# Initial Thought Dump

**Time needed:** 30-60 minutes
**Instructions:** Write freely without filtering or organizing. Don't worry about structure, grammar, or making sense. Just dump everything in your head.

---

## Part 1: Goals & Aspirations

*What do you want to do? What's on your mind? What are you working toward?*

**Prompt questions:**
- What projects/skills/experiences are you thinking about pursuing?
- What do you want to learn or get better at?
- What would you like to build, create, or launch?
- What experiences do you want to have?
- What milestones matter to you?

**Write here:**

```
[Your goals and aspirations - as many or as few as come to mind. No judgment, just dump everything.]

Examples:
- Learn 3D printing, buy a printer
- Take a ceramics course
- Launch new products for my business
- Travel more
- Change jobs to something more flexible
- Learn to drive
- etc.
```

---

## Part 2: Recent Accomplishments

*What have you already done? What are you proud of? What worked?*

**Prompt questions:**
- What did you accomplish in the past year?
- What moments felt most alive or meaningful?
- What did you create, build, or launch?
- What new experiences did you have?
- What relationships deepened?
- What internal growth happened (therapy, self-discovery, boundaries, etc.)?

**Write here:**

```
[Your accomplishments - big or small, external or internal. Include moments that mattered to you.]

Examples:
- Launched a new product line
- Took my family on a meaningful trip
- Started therapy and made progress
- Hit a milestone (subscribers, revenue, etc.)
- Tried something new (art form, sport, experience)
- Handled a difficult situation with courage
- etc.
```

---

## Part 3: Patterns You Notice

*What patterns do you see in your goals and accomplishments? What themes emerge?*

**Prompt questions:**
- What types of activities keep appearing?
- What energizes you vs. what drains you?
- What feels like "you" vs. what feels like "should"?
- What creates stress or overwhelm?
- What makes time disappear (in a good way)?
- What do you keep thinking about but not doing?

**Write here:**

```
[Patterns, themes, tensions, questions you have about yourself]

Examples:
- I want to do SO many things but feel overwhelmed
- I feel guilty when I'm not producing
- I'm drawn to both building things and experiencing things
- Work feels like it dominates everything
- I want multiple revenue streams (why?)
- I compare myself to others and feel "behind"
- I'm happiest when I'm moving/walking
- etc.
```

---

## Part 4: Current State

*How are you feeling right now about your life/work/goals?*

**Prompt questions:**
- What's working well?
- What's not working?
- What feels out of balance?
- What do you wish was different?
- What are you avoiding or procrastinating on?
- What keeps you up at night?

**Write here:**

```
[Your current reality - the good, the hard, the confusing]

Examples:
- I'm accomplishing things but feel exhausted
- I can't seem to say no to opportunities
- I want more time for family but work dominates
- I start things but don't finish them
- I feel like I should be further along
- I'm excited about many things but can't focus
- etc.
```

---

## Part 5: Random Brain Dump

*Anything else on your mind? Worries, ideas, questions, frustrations, hopes?*

**Write here:**

```
[Everything else that didn't fit above. Stream of consciousness is fine.]
```
